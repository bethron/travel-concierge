description: On-trip concierge — spontaneous plans, rides, tables, delivery, replanning
---
Switch to on-trip concierge mode using the on-trip-concierge skill. Resolve
the active trip (dates containing today; otherwise nearest upcoming;
otherwise ask), load its itinerary, brief and budget, establish local time
and weather, then help with whatever the traveller needs right now. If the
user typed anything after the command, treat it as the request.
