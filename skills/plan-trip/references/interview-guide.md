# Short Interview Guide

Ask only what the trip brief still lacks after the template and the traveller
profile are applied. Target: one round of at most 3 grouped questions, with
tappable options where the UI supports them.

## Question bank (in priority order)

1. **Purpose** — leisure or business? Skip if obvious from phrasing or the
   chosen template. Business switches to the business branch below.
2. **Destination** — skip if given. For "Surprise me", ask region preference
   (Europe / Asia / Americas / anywhere).
3. **Dates** — exact dates, or rough ("early September, about a week").
   Rough is fine; flight search can scan a window (Kiwi.com and Ryanair
   support cheapest-per-day searches).
4. **Origin** — home airport or city. Pull from traveller profile when set.
5. **Party** — solo / couple / family (ages of children) / group size.
6. **Total budget** — a ceiling and currency. Offer bands as options
   (e.g. "under £500", "£500–1,500", "£1,500–4,000", "no ceiling") but accept
   any figure. Record whether it includes flights.
7. **Pace** — packed days / balanced / plenty of downtime.
8. **Must-dos and avoids** — one open question: "Anything this trip must
   include — or definitely avoid?"

## Business branch

When the purpose is business, replace questions 5–8 with:

1. **Commitments** — meeting/conference dates, times and venue address(es).
   These anchor flights, stay and everything else.
2. **Policy** — any company cap (per night or total), preferred airlines or
   hotel chains, booking rules. Offer "no policy — use judgement" as an
   option.
3. **Receipts** — itemised expense log needed? If yes, `trip-budget` keeps
   every cost itemised with dates.
4. **Extend for leisure?** — one yes/no: add personal days before or after?
   If yes, run the short leisure questions (pace, interests) for those days
   only.

Loyalty programmes come from the traveller profile or Otto Travel — mention
which will be applied rather than asking again.

## Rules

- Never re-ask something already in the traveller profile or the message.
- Summarise the completed brief back in 2–3 sentences and get a nod before
  searching.
- If the traveller answers vaguely, adopt a sensible default, state it, and
  move on — do not loop on clarifications.
