# Quick-Start Trip Templates

Offer these as tappable options when a trip begins. Each template pre-fills
defaults so the interview stays short. State the assumptions made and let the
traveller override any of them.

| Template | Duration default | Pace | Emphasis | Typical picks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| City break | 2–4 nights | Brisk | Sights, food, museums | Central hotel, walkable plan, 1 headline activity/day |
| Beach & relax | 5–7 nights | Slow | Rest, sea, food | Resort or beachfront stay, 2–3 activities all trip |
| Adventure & outdoors | 5–10 nights | Active | Hiking, water sports, nature | Guided activities via Veltra, flexible stays |
| Culture & history | 4–7 nights | Moderate | Heritage sites, tours, local life | Guided tours, boutique/central stays |
| Food & drink | 3–5 nights | Moderate | Restaurants, markets, tastings | Food tours, cooking classes, central stay |
| Family holiday | 5–7 nights | Gentle | Kid-friendly, low logistics | Family rooms, short transfers, flexible mornings |
| Honeymoon / romantic | 5–10 nights | Slow | Special stays, memorable moments | Splurge stay, 1 signature experience |
| Backpacking / budget | 7–14 nights | Flexible | Value, spontaneity | Cheapest flights (Ryanair/Kiwi), budget stays, free sights |
| Multi-city | 7–14 nights | Brisk | 2–3 cities, transport between | Open-jaw flights, 2–3 night blocks per city |
| Surprise me | traveller's dates | Moderate | Balanced sampler | Balanced mix; propose 2 destination ideas if none given |

## Template application rules

- Templates set defaults for pace, stay type and activity density only —
  never budget level. Ask budget separately.
- Combine templates when the traveller mixes them ("relaxing city break") by
  taking the slower pace and the union of emphases.
- For "Surprise me" with no destination: ask origin, dates and budget, then
  propose two contrasting destinations with one-line pitches before searching.
