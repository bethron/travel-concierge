# Packing Library

Base checklists to prune and extend per trip. Never output all of these —
select what the destination, season, activities and party actually require.

## Universal core
- [ ] Passport / ID (+ visa or ETA confirmation if needed)
- [ ] Flight and accommodation confirmations (offline copies)
- [ ] Travel insurance details
- [ ] Payment cards + small amount of local cash
- [ ] Phone, charger, power bank
- [ ] Plug adapter (name the destination plug type)
- [ ] Medications + basic first-aid
- [ ] Toiletries (≤100 ml if cabin-only)
- [ ] Underwear/socks per day + 1
- [ ] Weather-appropriate outer layer

## Warm / beach add-ons
Swimwear ×2 · sunscreen (reef-safe if snorkelling) · sunglasses · hat ·
sandals · beach bag · after-sun · insect repellent

## Cold / winter add-ons
Thermal base layers · insulated jacket · hat, gloves, scarf · waterproof
boots · lip balm · hand warmers

## City break add-ons
Comfortable walking shoes · day bag with zips · smart-casual outfit ·
portable umbrella

## Outdoors / adventure add-ons
Hiking boots (worn in) · technical layers · waterproofs · daypack · water
bottle/bladder · blister plasters · headtorch · dry bag

## Family add-ons
Kids' documents · snacks · entertainment for transit · child medications ·
buggy/carrier decision · swim nappies if relevant

## Business / smart add-ons
Laptop + charger · smart outfits (count the occasions) · garment care ·
business cards

## Long-haul flight kit
Neck pillow · eye mask + earplugs · compression socks · empty water bottle ·
entertainment downloaded
